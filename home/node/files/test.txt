Full job description
Airworks Compressors Corp. and Airtek Systems Inc. are seeking a Digital Marketing & Content Designer to support digital marketing, brand management, and technical content creation across both companies.

This role blends digital marketing, graphic design, content creation, and technical communication. You will be responsible for developing high-quality marketing, sales, training, and internal materials while supporting e-commerce, SEO, and campaign execution.

This is an independent, hands-on role ideal for a self-starter who enjoys owning projects end-to-end and working closely with engineering, operations, and marketing teams.

Key Responsibilities

Digital Marketing & SEO

Develop and execute digital marketing campaigns across website, e-commerce, email, social media, and paid advertising
Manage and optimize e-commerce platforms to improve user experience and conversion
Conduct keyword research and implement SEO best practices
Track performance using Google Analytics and apply insights to improve results
Content Creation & Copywriting

Create and manage content for websites, blogs, social media, email campaigns, and sales materials
Write clear, engaging copy for marketing, training, and internal documentation
Translate technical concepts into easy-to-understand language
Maintain a consistent brand voice across all platforms
Graphic Design & Visual Content

Design marketing, sales, and training materials using Adobe Creative Suite
Create diagrams, assembly guides, and internal documentation
Develop simple animations or motion graphics for digital content
Prepare print-ready files and marketing assets
Brand Management

Maintain and evolve brand guidelines for Airworks and Airtek
Ensure consistent brand application across all materials
Support internal teams as a brand and content resource
Collaboration & Project Management

Work closely with marketing, sales, engineering, and operations teams
Collaborate with in-house videography and photography resources
Manage multiple projects and prioritize deadlines effectively
Qualifications

Required

Experience in digital marketing, content marketing, graphic design, or technical communication
Proficiency with WordPress and Adobe Creative Suite (InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator)
Working knowledge of SEO, Google Analytics, and email marketing platforms
Strong writing and editing skills
Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to work independently
Comfortable working in an industrial or manufacturing environment
Preferred

Experience with motion graphics or animation (After Effects or similar)
Experience adapting CAD or 3D models into visual content
Mechanical or technical aptitude
Experience in B2B, industrial, or manufacturing marketing
Why Join Us

Creative ownership in a growing technical company
Work on engineered products with real-world applications
Collaborative, in-house creative and technical teams
Opportunity for growth and advancement
